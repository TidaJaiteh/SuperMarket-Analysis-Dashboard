# SuperMarket-Analysis-Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supermarket sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and identify business opportunities through data-driven insights.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of:
- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Gross Income
- Average Customer Rating

### Product & Sales Analysis
Analyzes:
- Revenue by Product Line
- Quantity Sold by Product Line
- Gross Income by Product Line
- Revenue by Time Period

### Customer Insights
Explores:
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Customer Type
- Payment Method Analysis
- Customer Spending Patterns

## Key Insights

- Home and Lifestyle generated the highest revenue.
- Afternoon was the peak sales period.
- Credit Card generated the highest revenue among payment methods.
- Members spent more than Normal customers.
